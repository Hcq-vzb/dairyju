Concentrated Juice Processing Line

A concentrate line built around evaporation and aroma recovery, because concentrate without recovered aroma cannot be reconstituted into a product that tastes like juice.

Key Specifications

Feed capacity1,000 - 15,000 L per hour
Output Brix60-72 degrees Brix
EvaporatorFalling film or forced circulation, 2-4 effect
Aroma recoveryDistillation column, 100-150 fold concentrate
Thermal treatment80-95 degrees C before evaporation
StorageAseptic bag-in-drum, 200 L or 1,000 L
Vitamin retention≥ 85 percent with low-temperature evaporation

Aroma recovery is what makes concentrate usable

Evaporation strips volatile aroma compounds that give juice its fresh character. A distillation column captures these before evaporation and concentrates them 100-150 fold, so they can be dosed back during reconstitution.

Multiple effects cut energy use

A single-effect evaporator uses about 1 kg of steam per kg of water removed. A four-effect unit uses about 0.3 kg, which on a 10,000 L per hour feed is the difference between a viable and a marginal plant.

Aseptic drum filling for storage

Concentrate is stored at ambient for months. Aseptic bag-in-drum filling with a validated sterile path keeps it stable without refrigeration.

Typical Applications

  • Fruit processing plants in season — Concentrate lets a plant buy fruit at harvest and sell juice year-round.
  • Export of fruit solids — Concentrate removes most of the water, cutting freight cost dramatically compared with single-strength juice.
  • Beverage premix manufacturing — Standardised 65 Brix concentrate is the input for reconstituted juice and drinks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I recover aroma?

Yes for any product sold as juice. Without aroma recovery, reconstituted juice tastes flat and buyers discount it.

How many effects do I need?

Two for small plants, three to four above 5,000 L per hour feed. Energy saving is the deciding factor.

What is the storage life of concentrate?

Twelve to twenty-four months ambient in an aseptic drum, depending on Brix and acid.
