Evaporated and Condensed Milk Line

An evaporated milk line where multi-effect evaporation and in-can sterilisation produce the caramelised colour and viscosity that define the product.

Key Specifications

Capacity2,000 - 30,000 L per hour feed
SolidsEvaporated 7.5-9.5 percent fat, condensed 28 percent total solids
Evaporator3-5 effect falling film
SterilisationIn-can 118 degrees C for 15 minutes, or UHT plus aseptic
Sugar additionDry or liquid, before or after evaporation
PackagingTin can, tube, or aseptic bag
Shelf life12-24 months ambient

In-can sterilisation creates the character

Evaporated milk gets its slight caramel colour and cooked flavour from in-can sterilisation at 118 degrees C. A UHT plus aseptic route produces a paler, fresher product that some markets reject, so the choice is a market decision, not an efficiency one.

Sugar timing affects viscosity

Adding sugar before evaporation increases viscosity and reduces heat transfer, so most plants add it as a dry blend after evaporation or as a concentrated syrup. Getting this wrong shows up as either a burnt evaporator or an unstable emulsion.

Homogenisation and stability

Evaporated milk is homogenised after evaporation at 200-250 bar to prevent fat separation during the long ambient storage. Skipping it produces a fat layer that shakes out but never fully redisperses.

Typical Applications

  • Markets with limited cold chain — Ambient stable milk concentrate is a staple where refrigeration is not universal.
  • Bakery and confectionery supply — Condensed milk is an ingredient for industrial users, sold in drums as well as cans.
  • Coffee and tea creamers — Evaporated milk with adjusted fat is the base for many creamer formulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

In-can or aseptic?

In-can for traditional evaporated milk character and tin packaging; aseptic for bag-in-box or when a lighter colour is preferred.

What is the difference from sweetened condensed milk?

Sweetened condensed milk relies on high sugar for preservation and is not sterilised; evaporated milk is sterilised and unsweetened.

Can the line run both products?

Yes with a recipe change, though the sugar dosing system is specified separately.
