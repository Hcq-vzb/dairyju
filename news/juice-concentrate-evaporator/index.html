Juice Concentrate Evaporator

Published 2026-11-06 · KIWL Machine

Most disagreements about a juice concentrate evaporator come down to what was never written down. This guide sets out the dairy and juice processors requirements that decide whether a machine works: what it must deliver at 5285–6785 units per hour, what it is built from, and what to verify before handover.

What This Equipment Does

The value of a juice concentrate evaporator is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 5285–6785 units per hour on 330 ml product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a juice concentrate evaporator on the specification that limits your output: the 0.3 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Before ordering a juice concentrate evaporator, write the interface list and assign an owner to each item: encoder, safety relay, product detection, cleaning return. This configuration already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare a juice concentrate evaporator against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 5285 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a dairy and juice processors operation.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 5285–6785 units per hour the juice concentrate evaporator accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the juice concentrate evaporator be upgraded later?

Within the 5285–6785 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 330 ml range before ordering.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for dairy and juice processors operations running audits.

Does the juice concentrate evaporator need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
