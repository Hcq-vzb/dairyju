What This Equipment Does

Filling accuracy compounds across the shift: a 0.3 ml deviation on a 500 ml container translates into measurable give-away at 9187–12687 bottles per hour, so dose repeatability usually matters more than headline speed.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a dairy filling and sealing machine contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 0.3 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact-part grade, the 28–39 kW load and the 1800 × 3600 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A dairy filling and sealing machine earns its integration cost by removing waiting. The buffer, the transfer and the 9187–12687 units per hour handshake determine whether the machine is the constraint or the answer — and this build accepts the standard interfaces that settle it.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A dairy filling and sealing machine handling 9187–12687 bottles per hour with ±0.3 ml tolerance attacks all three for a dairy and juice processors operation.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this dairy filling and sealing machine are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the dairy filling and sealing machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 500 ml product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

Does the dairy filling and sealing machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 3600 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

Can the dairy filling and sealing machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 9187–12687 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 500 ml range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
