Fruit Pulper Machine

Published 2026-10-31 · KIWL Machine

There is no universal fruit pulper machine for dairy and juice processors. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this fruit pulper machine it is: it processes 500 ml product at 2954–3954 units per hour, consistently, without the dairy and juice processors team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a fruit pulper machine on the specification that limits your output: the 1.0 ml tolerance, the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 8-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The fruit pulper machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the dairy and juice processors workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the fruit pulper machine against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 2954–3954 units per hour a typical dairy and juice processors operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a fruit pulper machine is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 2954–3954 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the fruit pulper machine?

The reference build draws 6.5–8.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Does the fruit pulper machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 1400 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for dairy and juice processors operations running audits.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
