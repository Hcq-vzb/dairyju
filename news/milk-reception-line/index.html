Milk Reception Line

Published 2026-11-25 · KIWL Machine

There is no universal milk reception line for dairy and juice processors. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

The milk reception line is where raw material becomes a finished unit, and that conversion is where a dairy and juice processors operation's cost is set. At 12282–16282 units per hour for 8 oz product, this build makes that conversion repeatable.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference milk reception line uses 304/316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 15–22 kW connected load and a 1800 × 2200 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This milk reception line takes a standard encoder signal and a 1800 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a milk reception line is arithmetic: every unit of 1.0 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 10-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the dairy and juice processors production line.

Maintenance and Service

For a dairy and juice processors operation the maintenance question for a milk reception line is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Is the milk reception line suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 12282–16282 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the milk reception line handle a 8 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
