Butter Churn Machine

Published 2026-11-10 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a butter churn machine for dairy and juice processors is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

A dairy and juice processors production line gains nothing from a butter churn machine that performs well on paper. This build is specified at 1632–2282 units per hour for 250–500 ml product with the deviation defined, so the performance can be measured rather than assumed.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 1632–2282 units per hour means little without 250–500 ml product, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover — all three stated for this butter churn machine.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The butter churn machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the dairy and juice processors workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A butter churn machine with a 10-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a dairy and juice processors buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

For a dairy and juice processors operation the maintenance question for a butter churn machine is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the butter churn machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Does the butter churn machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 1900 × 3200 mm GA drawing.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
