Milk Homogenizer

Published 2026-11-02 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a milk homogenizer for dairy and juice processors is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a milk homogenizer is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 1979 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the dairy and juice processors production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference milk homogenizer uses 316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 30–45 kW connected load and a 1200 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A milk homogenizer fits an existing dairy and juice processors production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a milk homogenizer is arithmetic before it is technical: 0.3 ml per unit, 1979–2479 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional dairy and juice processors scale.

Maintenance and Service

The milk homogenizer keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 1979 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the milk homogenizer?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 12 oz set is included.

Is a 316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 12 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What does the milk homogenizer do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
