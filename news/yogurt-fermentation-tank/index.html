Yogurt Fermentation Tank

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machine

Where a yogurt fermentation tank specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the dairy and juice processors requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a yogurt fermentation tank removes from the dairy and juice processors process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 330 ml product at 1629–2129 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A yogurt fermentation tank with a 8-minute changeover across 330 ml format and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 1629–2129 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 1.5 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 1629–2129 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A yogurt fermentation tank with a 10-inch colour touch and a 8-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a dairy and juice processors buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Put the yogurt fermentation tank on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 1629–2129 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the yogurt fermentation tank?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1629 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Is the yogurt fermentation tank suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1629–2129 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

What does the yogurt fermentation tank do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
