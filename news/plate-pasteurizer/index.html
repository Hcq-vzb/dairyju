Plate Pasteurizer

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Machine

A plate pasteurizer for dairy and juice processors is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

A plate pasteurizer earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1665–2315 units per hour for 5 L product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference plate pasteurizer uses 304 stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 6.5–8.5 kW connected load and a 3200 × 2800 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the dairy and juice processors team will adopt the routine — and a plate pasteurizer with a 8-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare a plate pasteurizer against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 1665 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a dairy and juice processors operation.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this plate pasteurizer the wear items are simple to identify — seals, drive, guide rails — and most dairy and juice processors downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the plate pasteurizer need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 2800 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

What does the plate pasteurizer do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How long does a format changeover take on this plate pasteurizer?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 5 L format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
