Juice Sterilization System

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machine

Where a juice sterilization system specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the dairy and juice processors requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

A juice sterilization system earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 8866–11866 units per hour for 1 L product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a juice sterilization system is usually changeover. At 15 minutes per 1 L format and 8866–11866 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This juice sterilization system takes a standard encoder signal and a 3200 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a juice sterilization system is downtime avoided during a 15-minute format change on a 1 L format, because the dairy and juice processors production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

A juice sterilization system that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the juice sterilization system do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

What is the actual connected load of the juice sterilization system?

The reference build draws 5.5–7.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
