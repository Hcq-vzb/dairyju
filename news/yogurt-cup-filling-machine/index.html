Yogurt Cup Filling Machine

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a yogurt cup filling machine. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the dairy and juice processors production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

A yogurt cup filling machine earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 2170–3170 units per hour for 330 ml can product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two yogurt cup filling machine quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies 304 stainless steel surfaces, a 7-inch colour touch, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 20-minute changeover across 330 ml can format.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a yogurt cup filling machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the dairy and juice processors production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a yogurt cup filling machine is arithmetic: every unit of 1.5 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 20-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the dairy and juice processors production line.

Maintenance and Service

The yogurt cup filling machine was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 2170 unit per hour dairy and juice processors production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a dairy and juice processors operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

What accuracy can this yogurt cup filling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±1.5 ml on 330 ml can containers at 2170–3170 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

What is the actual connected load of the yogurt cup filling machine?

The reference build draws 5.5–7.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
