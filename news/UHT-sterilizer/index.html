UHT Sterilizer

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same dairy and juice processors figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

Because an UHT sterilizer handles a defined step for dairy and juice processors, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 500 ml product at 17956–22456 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for an UHT sterilizer contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 0.3 ml tolerance, the 304/316L stainless steel contact-part grade, the 30–45 kW load and the 2400 × 2200 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

An UHT sterilizer fits an existing dairy and juice processors production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 2400 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the UHT sterilizer on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 500 ml product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Insist on a spare parts list with lead times when the UHT sterilizer is quoted. A 304/316L stainless steel contact-part specification without a parts commitment is only half a specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the UHT sterilizer handle a 500 ml product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

Does the UHT sterilizer need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 2200 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

Can the UHT sterilizer run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The dairy and juice processors team still owns the routine checks, but the 10-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
