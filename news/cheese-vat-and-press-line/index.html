Cheese Vat And Press Line

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

Where a cheese vat and press line specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the dairy and juice processors requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

A dairy and juice processors production line gains nothing from a cheese vat and press line that performs well on paper. This build is specified at 1590–2240 units per hour for 1 L product with the deviation defined, so the performance can be measured rather than assumed.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a cheese vat and press line contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 2.0 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact-part grade, the 17–24 kW load and the 1200 × 2800 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the cheese vat and press line does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1200 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing dairy and juice processors conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A cheese vat and press line at 1590–2240 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 1 L product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the dairy and juice processors operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this cheese vat and press line are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Can the cheese vat and press line be upgraded later?

Within the 1590–2240 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 1 L range before ordering.

Does the cheese vat and press line need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 2800 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
