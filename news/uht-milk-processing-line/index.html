UHT Milk Processing Line

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

Choosing the right uht milk processing line is a practical decision for dairy and juice processors. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting an uht milk processing line on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

An uht milk processing line occupies one defined station in the production line for dairy and juice processors. This build runs at 18069–22569 units per hour for 250–500 ml product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. An uht milk processing line with a 8-minute changeover across 250–500 ml format and 304 stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 18069–22569 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating an uht milk processing line as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1600 mm working width, it becomes part of the dairy and juice processors control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for an uht milk processing line is the downtime it prevents during a 8-minute changeover. At 18069 units per hour, the dairy and juice processors production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 18069–22569 units per hour the uht milk processing line accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the uht milk processing line do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How long does a format changeover take on this uht milk processing line?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 250–500 ml format takes longer.

Is the uht milk processing line suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 18069–22569 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
