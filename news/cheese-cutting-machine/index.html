Cheese Cutting Machine

Published 2026-11-10 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a cheese cutting machine for dairy and juice processors is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a cheese cutting machine performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 1 L product and delivers 8428–11928 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

For a dairy and juice processors buyer the cheese cutting machine specification worth writing down is: 8428–11928 units per hour, 1 L format, 0.5 % tolerance, 7-inch colour touch controls, SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and a 12-minute changeover.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a cheese cutting machine is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the dairy and juice processors production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include spares and the service arrangement in the numbers. A cheese cutting machine with a 7-inch colour touch and a 12-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 8428–11928 units per hour the cheese cutting machine accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the cheese cutting machine behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the cheese cutting machine determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Can the cheese cutting machine run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The dairy and juice processors team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

How is the cheese cutting machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
