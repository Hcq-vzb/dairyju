Curd Whey Separator

Published 2026-11-05 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a curd whey separator for dairy and juice processors is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

Position a curd whey separator by what it removes from the dairy and juice processors process. This configuration handles 250–500 ml product at 2480–3480 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a curd whey separator on the specification that limits your output: the 3.0 ml tolerance, the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 12-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The curd whey separator is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the dairy and juice processors workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the curd whey separator against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 2480–3480 units per hour a typical dairy and juice processors operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the curd whey separator is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 2480–3480 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the curd whey separator suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 2480–3480 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Does the curd whey separator need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 1400 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

What is the delivery lead time for the curd whey separator?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 250–500 ml set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
