Milk Cooling Tank

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machine

The milk cooling tank is easier to specify than most dairy and juice processors buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a milk cooling tank removes from the dairy and juice processors process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 1 L product at 1981–2481 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a milk cooling tank specification for what is measurable: rate 1981–2481 units per hour, deviation ±0.3 ml, container 1 L, control 4-inch HMI, load 1.5–2.5 kW, footprint 1600 × 2200 × 3200 mm, changeover 12 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The milk cooling tank is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the dairy and juice processors workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A milk cooling tank rated at 1981–2481 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Insist on a spare parts list with lead times when the milk cooling tank is quoted. A 304/316L stainless steel contact-part specification without a parts commitment is only half a specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the milk cooling tank suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1981–2481 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

What spare parts should be held for the milk cooling tank?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1981 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for dairy and juice processors operations running audits.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
