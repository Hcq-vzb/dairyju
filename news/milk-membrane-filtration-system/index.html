Milk Membrane Filtration System

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

A milk membrane filtration system sits on the critical path of the dairy and juice processors production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a milk membrane filtration system.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this milk membrane filtration system it is: it processes 20 L product at 1883–2383 units per hour, consistently, without the dairy and juice processors team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

For a dairy and juice processors buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: 316L stainless steel product-contact parts, 4-inch HMI controls, 2.0 ml tolerance, 10-minute changeover, 1.5–2.5 kW connected load and a 2400 × 2800 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A milk membrane filtration system that shares its control protocol with the rest of the dairy and juice processors production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the milk membrane filtration system to real 20 L volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 1883–2383 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this milk membrane filtration system the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 10-minute routine service realistic on a 1883 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this milk membrane filtration system hold in real production?

The reference build holds 2.0 ml on 20 L product at 1883–2383 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the milk membrane filtration system handle a 20 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

What is the actual connected load of the milk membrane filtration system?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
