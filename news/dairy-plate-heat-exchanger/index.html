Dairy Plate Heat Exchanger

Published 2026-11-22 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a dairy plate heat exchanger for dairy and juice processors. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

A dairy plate heat exchanger occupies one defined station in the production line for dairy and juice processors. This build runs at 5560–7060 units per hour for 1.5 L product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two dairy plate heat exchanger quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies 316L stainless steel surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 0.3 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover across 1.5 L format.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the dairy and juice processors team will adopt the routine — and a dairy plate heat exchanger with a 15-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A dairy plate heat exchanger rated at 5560–7060 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the dairy plate heat exchanger service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the dairy plate heat exchanger?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 1.5 L set is included.

What does the dairy plate heat exchanger do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

What spare parts should be held for the dairy plate heat exchanger?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 5560 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
