Dairy Bottle Unscrambler

Published 2026-11-17 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a dairy bottle unscrambler for dairy and juice processors. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

What a dairy bottle unscrambler does to the dairy and juice processors production line is measurable at the packing end. At 7889–10889 units per hour for 1.5 L product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference dairy bottle unscrambler uses SUS304 with sanitary fittings for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 5.5–7.5 kW connected load and a 2400 × 1400 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the dairy bottle unscrambler can hand over at 7889–10889 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a dairy bottle unscrambler is downtime avoided during a 8-minute format change on a 1.5 L format, because the dairy and juice processors production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

Insist on a spare parts list with lead times when the dairy bottle unscrambler is quoted. A SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact-part specification without a parts commitment is only half a specification.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for dairy and juice processors operations running audits.

Is the dairy bottle unscrambler suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 7889–10889 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 1.5 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
