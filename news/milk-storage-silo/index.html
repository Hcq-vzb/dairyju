Milk Storage Silo

Published 2026-11-09 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a milk storage silo for dairy and juice processors. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a milk storage silo is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 990 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the dairy and juice processors production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a milk storage silo contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 2.0 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact-part grade, the 1.5–2.5 kW load and the 2400 × 2800 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A milk storage silo becomes part of the dairy and juice processors production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A milk storage silo at 990–1340 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 12 oz product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the dairy and juice processors operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this milk storage silo the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 8-minute routine service realistic on a 990 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the milk storage silo behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the milk storage silo determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Can the milk storage silo handle a 12 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
