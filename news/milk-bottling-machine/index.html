Milk Bottling Machine

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machine

Where a milk bottling machine specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the dairy and juice processors requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

A milk bottling machine earns a place on the dairy and juice processors production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 6022–9022 units per hour on 20 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a milk bottling machine on the specification that limits your output: the 1.5 ml tolerance, the 304/316L stainless steel contact parts and the 8-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The milk bottling machine is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the dairy and juice processors workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the milk bottling machine to real 20 L volume, then let the 1.5 ml tolerance and the 6022–9022 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Put the milk bottling machine on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 6022–9022 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this milk bottling machine hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±1.5 ml on 20 L containers at 6022–9022 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the milk bottling machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 20 L product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

Does the milk bottling machine need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 2800 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
